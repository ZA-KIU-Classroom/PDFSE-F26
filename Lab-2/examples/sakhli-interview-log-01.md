# Interview 01 · Host, 6 rooms, Oni · (code name: Oni-1)

**Week:** 2 · **Asker:** LB · **Logger:** MG · **Consent:** yes; notes only, no recording
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** Runs a 6-room family guesthouse in Oni with her son's help. Lists on Booking.com; most repeat guests call or WhatsApp her. Introduced through Keti's family, so Luka asked the questions and Keti stayed out of the room.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if hosts do not raise the commission themselves before we mention it.

**Quotes** (Georgian as spoken; English beside it; ★ = surprise)
- "როცა შვილი თბილისშია, არავინ პასუხობს." / "When my son is in Tbilisi, nobody answers."
- "აგვისტოში არ მძინავს. ღამის სამ საათზე ტელეფონს ვამოწმებ." / "In August I don't sleep. I check the phone at three in the morning." ★
- "ორჯერ ერთი და იგივე ოთახი ორ ოჯახს მივეცი." / "Twice I gave the same room to two families." ★
- "ყველაფერს ამ რვეულში ვწერ." / "I write everything in this notebook." (she showed it: one page per week, crossings-out in pen)
- "ყველა იხდის. ეს სტუმრების ფასია." / "Everyone pays it. It is the price of guests." (only after Mariam asked what Booking.com costs her)
- "ვინმემ ამისთვის აპლიკაცია უნდა გააკეთოს." / "Someone should make an app for this." (feature talk: logged, not counted as evidence)

**Workaround:** A paper notebook by the phone, one page per week. Her son writes the English replies when he is home.
**Cost:** Two double-bookings last season. One family refunded and put up at a neighbor's at her expense.
**Frequency:** Requests on three channels in the same hour, most evenings in July and August.
**Prediction check:** No. She never raised the commission until we asked, and then called it normal.
**Surprise ★:** The fear is double-booking and the 3 a.m. phone checks, not the commission. Commission is a cost she has accepted; the double-booking is a shame she still carries.
**Commitment:** Introduced two neighbors who also host; agreed we can come back when we have something to show.
**Next:** Ask the neighbors about the last time they worried about a double-booking. Candidate question for script v2.

Written within 24 hours. Luka read it the same evening and added the notebook quote Mariam had missed.

---

## What to notice

- **Quotes in the language spoken, translation beside.** A translated quote is already a paraphrase; the original is the evidence.
- **The commission quote is labeled "only after Mariam asked".** Without that label, the same words would look like support for the prediction.
- **"Someone should make an app" is logged, then disarmed.** A feature request is a compliment in disguise.
