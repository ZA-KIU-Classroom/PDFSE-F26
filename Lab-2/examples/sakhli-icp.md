# Ideal Customer Profile

**Name (invented) and one-line sketch:** Nino, 52, runs a 6-room family guesthouse in Oni.

**Their situation:** She lists on Booking.com and prints a WhatsApp number on a card she hands every guest. Some guests call her directly, some write on Instagram. She speaks Georgian and Russian and very little English; her son answers English messages when he is home. Summer is chaos, winter is empty.

**The moment the problem shows up:** July and August evenings, when requests arrive on Booking.com, WhatsApp, and the phone within the same hour and she has to remember which room she already promised.

**What it costs them:** Two double-bookings last season. One family arrived with suitcases to a full house; she refunded them and paid for a room elsewhere. She still apologizes when she tells the story.

**What they do about it today:** A paper notebook by the phone with every booking written by hand, plus her son's help with English replies when he is not in Tbilisi.

**What they are NOT:**
- Hotels with a front desk and booking software: they already have a system and a staff member to run it.
- Owners renting one apartment on Airbnb: one unit on one channel cannot be double-booked across channels.

**How we reach five of them this week:**
| Person or channel | Who on our team | Contacted? |
|---|---|---|
| Keti's aunt, host in Oni | Keti (Luka asks: a relative answers to please family) | Yes, Saturday booked |
| Dato, host next door to her, Oni | Keti | Yes, same visit |
| Three hosts in Ambrolauri, through Mariam's uncle | Mariam | Uncle messaged; waiting for names |

**Evidence so far:** `00-foundation/problem-pool.md`, Keti's statements 1 to 3 (the aunt's two double-bookings; the son writing English replies; the notebook). After interviews: links to logs go here.

This is v1. It will change after your first three interviews, and that is the point.
