# Problem Hypothesis v1

## Step 1: What each of us believes
| Teammate | What I believe the real problem is |
|---|---|
| Keti | Booking.com's commission eats the summer profit. |
| Luka | Hosts are overwhelmed in July and August. |
| Mariam | They cannot answer foreign guests fast enough in English. |

## Step 2: The hypothesis we test first
> **We believe** family guesthouse hosts in Racha with 3 to 10 rooms, like Nino in Oni,
> **struggle with** the commission Booking.com takes on every booking
> **when** they look at their payouts during peak season,
> **which costs them** 15 to 18 percent of their summer revenue.
> **Today they** accept it, because Booking.com is where the foreign guests are.
> **We are wrong if** hosts do not raise the commission themselves when they describe their biggest problems, before we mention it.

## Step 3: The beliefs we did not pick
- Summer overload (Luka) → script question 6: "Tell me about the busiest week you had last summer."
- Answering foreign guests (Mariam) → script question 7: "Walk me through the last message you got from a guest abroad. What did you do?"

## Checks before you commit
- [x] No product words (app, platform, tool, AI, bot)
- [x] "When" is a moment, not "sometimes" or "when busy"
- [x] "We are wrong if" could be seen in a single interview log
- [x] The "we are wrong if" line is copied to the top of your script and your log template

## After interviews (filled in Week 3; shown so you see where this ends. In your repo it stays empty until Lab 3.)
**Verdict:** killed · **Evidence:** `interview-logs/01-host-oni.md` to `04-host-ambrolauri.md`: no host raised the commission before being asked; all four raised double-booking on their own. Recorded in `DECISIONS.md` as the second line. Hypothesis v2 is about double-booking.
