# Worked Example: Sakhli Interview Script v1 (the finished version)

The Sakhli team's script after the Lab 2 audit, as used in their first real interviews. Copy the **shape**, never the questions. How it was built: `resources/interview-script.md`.

---

**Problem we are investigating:** how family guesthouse hosts in Racha get and manage bookings
**Our prediction (from problem-hypothesis.md):** We are wrong if hosts do not raise the commission themselves before we mention it.
**Asker:** Keti · **Logger:** Mariam · **Language:** Georgian (English beside it in the repo)

## Opening (2 min)
"Thank you for making time. I'm Keti, a student at KIU. We're learning how family guesthouses handle bookings day to day. I'm not selling anything and I don't have a product to show you. Is it okay if my teammate takes notes? We won't use your name anywhere."

## Warm-up (2 min)
1. Tell me about your guesthouse. How did you start hosting?

## Core (12 to 15 min)
2. Walk me through the last booking you took, from the very first message.
3. Tell me about the last time a booking went wrong.
4. What did that cost you?
5. What do you use to keep track of who is arriving? *(then: can you show me?)*
6. Tell me about the busiest week you had last summer. *(Luka's belief: overload)*
7. Walk me through the last message you got from a guest abroad. What did you do? *(Mariam's belief: language)*

## Close (2 min)
8. Who else in the valley should we talk to about this?
9. Could we come back when we have something to show you?

## Follow-up bank (glance here when stuck)
- And then what happened?
- Why was that a problem?
- When was the last time that happened? *(after "usually" or "always")*
- Can you show me?
- What would that let you do that you can't now? *(after a feature request)*
- *Silence. Count to three.*
