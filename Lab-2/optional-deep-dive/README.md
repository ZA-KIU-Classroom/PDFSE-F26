# Optional Deep Dive

Stretch material. Never required, never graded.

- **The Mom Test, Rob Fitzpatrick.** Finish the whole book. Chapters 4 to 7 cover keeping interviews casual, commitment and advancement, and finding people to talk to.
- **Jobs to Be Done, the switch interview.** Bob Moesta's work (for example *Demand-Side Sales 101*) on interviewing people about the moment they switched from one solution to another. A sharper tool once you have a problem and want to understand why people change behavior.
- **Continuous Discovery Habits, Teresa Torres.** The chapters on interviewing and the opportunity solution tree. Week 3's reading goes deeper here.
- **Loftus and Palmer (1974), "Reconstruction of automobile destruction".** The original memory study behind the 24-hour logging rule. Short and readable.
