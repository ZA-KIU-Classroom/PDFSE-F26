# Lab 2 · Pick, Profile, Script
**Week 2 · Starts the moment Lecture 2 ends · 2 hours**

This page is the whole lab. Each step links a concept guide in `resources/` for when you want to go deeper: you can finish without them, and you will do it better with them.

## What you leave with
A team repo, one problem to investigate, a person-shaped ICP, a written problem hypothesis, an interview script that survived another team's audit, a practice interview each, and three real interviews booked. This is the core of Milestone 1 (10 pts, Week 4).

## Bring
Your team and your Lab 1 problem pool. Everything else was in the lecture you just left.

| Clock | What happens |

Teams were due at the start of today's lecture. No team? Tell me in the first ten minutes. Contracts lock at the end of this lab.

## C0 (by 0:30): Team repo live
One person creates the repo while the team watches. Follow [`TEAM-REPO-SETUP.md`](../TEAM-REPO-SETUP.md) at the root of the course repo exactly: name, collaborators, the professor (`ZA-KIU`), the folder structure for the whole semester, your Lab 1 files moved in, and one first commit from every member's own account. Post the link in your lab group's Teams channel.

## C1 (by 0:45): Pick one problem to investigate
Copy `templates/four-filters-scorecard.md` into `00-foundation/`. Shortlist 3 problems from your pool, score each filter with evidence, pick one. You pick it to **investigate**, not to build: your interviews may still kill it. Write the pick as the first line of `DECISIONS.md` and name your runner-up. The Evidence Rule starts now.

Stuck between two? [`resources/four-filters.md`](resources/four-filters.md) · Example: `examples/sakhli-four-filters-scorecard.md`

## C2 (by 1:05): ICP and problem hypothesis
**ICP (10 min).** Copy `templates/icp.md` into `00-foundation/icp.md`. One person, not a segment, and five of them you can reach this week. Help: [`resources/writing-an-icp.md`](resources/writing-an-icp.md) · Example: `examples/sakhli-icp.md`

**Hypothesis (10 min).** Copy `templates/problem-hypothesis.md` into `01-discovery/`. Each teammate writes their own belief first, silently. Then pick one to test and fill the six slots, ending with **"we are wrong if..."**. Commit it now, before any real interview. Help: [`resources/problem-hypothesis.md`](resources/problem-hypothesis.md) · Example: `examples/sakhli-problem-hypothesis.md`

## C3 (by 1:25): Script v1, then an audit
Copy `templates/interview-script-v1.md` into `01-discovery/`. Your prediction goes at the top: the "we are wrong if" line. Build the core on the lecture skeleton, plus one question for each belief you did not pick. Then swap with the team I pair you with: score every question green, amber, or red. Fix every red.

Full step-by-step: [`resources/interview-script.md`](resources/interview-script.md). A finished script to copy the shape from: `examples/sakhli-interview-script-v1.md`.

## Practice round (1:25 to 1:40)
Pair with the team that audited you. Seven minutes each way: interview them about their own life with your fixed script while a teammate logs it from `templates/interview-log.md` into `01-discovery/interview-logs/practice/`. Your interviewee names one green moment and one red. Before your first real interview, read [`resources/running-and-logging-interviews.md`](resources/running-and-logging-interviews.md). A finished real log: `examples/sakhli-interview-log-01.md`.

## Studio (1:40 to 1:50): Book three real interviews
Open `01-discovery/outreach-tracker.md` (moved in at C0). Send messages until three interviews have a day and time: their world, twenty minutes, two time options.
