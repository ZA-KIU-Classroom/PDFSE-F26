## The strongest incumbent

The paper notebook. It appears in 4 of 8 logs, nobody complained about the notebook itself, only about what it cannot see, and it costs nothing, never runs out of battery, and the host trusts her own handwriting. Anything we build has to beat the notebook at the moment a request arrives, not beat Booking.com. (03, 05)

## Alternatives nobody mentioned

- **Airbnb.** We assumed it was a big player in Racha. Zero of 8 hosts named it. It stays off the table until a log says otherwise.
- **Channel managers** (software that syncs calendars across sites). Found online. No host had heard of one, so it is not a competitor for this person yet.

## What to notice

- **Five of the eight rows are not software.** A notebook, a son, a neighbor, a cousin, memory. These are where the job actually lives today.
- **Booking.com is in the table but is not the strongest incumbent.** The team expected to fight Booking.com. The logs say the fight is with the notebook.
- **The "nobody mentioned" section ends arguments.** When a teammate says "but Airbnb," the answer is in the file.
