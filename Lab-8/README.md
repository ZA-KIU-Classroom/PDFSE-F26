# Lab 8: First Real-Data Review

## What you leave this lab with

Four things, at exactly these paths.

| # | What | Where | Starts from |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Field-week readout: prediction, reality, surprise, what changes, who is next | `03-build/analytics/break-readout.md` | `templates/break-readout.md` |
| 2 | Experiment 1: six parts, frozen, launched or scheduled | `03-build/experiments/experiment-1.md` | `templates/experiment-1.md` |
| 3 | One decision: what the readout changes | Bottom of `DECISIONS.md` | Last section of `templates/break-readout.md` |
| 4 | Messages to your next users, sent in the lab | Section 8 of the readout | Your draft from the Week 8 session |

`examples/` holds Sakhli's version of each. The file is named `break-readout.md` because the repo map from Lab 2 says so; it holds your field week.

## Three states, one lab

- **A: real people used it.** Read your events.
- **B: live, nobody came.** Your field table is your data. Read where each link stopped.
- **C: gate still open.** Your rung is the finding. Checkpoint 1 is yours for closing it; write the readout's sections 2, 5 and 8 anyway.

## Before you arrive

Watch-check submitted. Sprint 2 file open at the prediction. Dashboard and database open. Field table's Opened column filled in honestly. Tonight's message drafted.

## 0:00 to 0:15. Prediction first

Every team types its state (A, B or C), its committed prediction, and the real number. Then two random teams share a dashboard or a field table.

## 0:15 to 0:35. Live demo

I open a volunteer team's real dashboard cold and say what it tells me and what it refuses to tell me.

## 0:35 to 1:00. Checkpoint 1: the readout

Copy `templates/break-readout.md` to `03-build/analytics/break-readout.md`. In order: reconcile the whole week; paste your prediction beside the real numbers; write your journey as step counts and mark the widest drop; name who is standing there; list what the data cannot tell you and who you will ask. Counts, not percentages. Then one thing that comes off your plan and one that goes on, and the DECISIONS.md line. Commit.

Optional: `resources/reading-a-first-week-of-data.md` if you do not know where to start; `resources/small-n-honesty.md` if you are about to write a percentage; `resources/when-nobody-came.md` for states B and C.

## 1:00 to 1:25. Checkpoint 2: Experiment 1

Copy `templates/experiment-1.md` to `03-build/experiments/experiment-1.md`. Baseline first. Then hypothesis, one change, metric and where it is read, success threshold, failure threshold, decision rule including "too little data". Prefer a change a teammate can do by hand this week. Commit before you start it: the commit is the freeze.

Optional: `resources/experiments-that-can-fail.md` if your failure threshold has no number in it.

## 1:25 to 1:45. Checkpoint 3: who is next

Fill section 8 of the readout: your field table as a funnel, then your next people by code name, the channel your own evidence supports, and who sends. Send the messages now. Type SENT in the chat. Commit.

Optional: `resources/recruiting-the-next-ten.md` if your message has an adjective in it.

## 1:45 to 1:50. Studio

Name your AI feature for Lab 9: one DECISIONS.md line (shape at the bottom of `templates/eval-set.md`). State C: update your Sprint 2 gate table.
