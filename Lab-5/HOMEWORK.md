## Running the sessions

Read the task from your test plan word for word, then stop talking. One teammate watches, one writes. Time to first tap, time to the one action, what they said without being asked, and the verdict against your own rule. The tester is from your ICP and not in this course; a teammate or classmate does not count as a session.

After three sessions, apply the decision rule you wrote in Lab 4. If it says change course, write the change as a DECISIONS.md line with the session numbers as evidence. That line is what Lab 6 starts from.
