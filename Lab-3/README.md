# Lab 3: First Synthesis

## What you leave this lab with

Four files in your team repo, at exactly these paths. Nothing else is produced today.

| # | File | Starts from |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01-discovery/synthesis/affinity-map.md` | `templates/affinity-map.md` |
| 2 | `01-discovery/synthesis/patterns-analysis.md` | `templates/patterns-analysis.md` |
| 3 | `DECISIONS.md`, one new line: your prediction's verdict | `templates/prediction-trial.md` (a worksheet; only its last line goes in your repo) |
| 4 | `01-discovery/interview-script-v2.md` | `templates/interview-script-v2.md` |

Each template says at the top where its file goes. Each one has a finished Sakhli version in `examples/` with the same name. Copy the shape, never the content.

## Before you arrive

Your real interview logs from the Lab 2 homework (3 or more), open. Your `01-discovery/problem-hypothesis.md` and `01-discovery/interview-script-v1.md`, open. The Week 3 recording watched and its watch-check submitted.

## 0:00 to 0:15. Recap and two demos

Two students picked at random screen-share their team's `01-discovery/interview-logs/` folder for two minutes each. Not a presentation; just show the files.

## 0:15 to 0:35. Live demo

I open a volunteer team's logs cold and audit them on screen: quotes versus paraphrase, surprise line present or missing. Then I cluster their quotes into a first affinity map live, mistakes included.

## 0:35 to 0:45. Checkpoint 1: log audit

Open every real log. Each one needs three things: 3 or more verbatim quotes, one line on what surprised you (if nothing did, write "nothing surprised us", that is still data), and consent recorded. A log with paraphrase instead of quotes: rewrite it now from your notes. Commit the fixed logs.

## 0:45 to 1:15. Checkpoint 2: affinity map and patterns

Copy `templates/affinity-map.md` to `01-discovery/synthesis/affinity-map.md`. Pull every quote from every real log into it, then group the ones that feel alike. Name each group with a sentence that says what the evidence shows, not a one-word topic. A group needs quotes from 2 or more interviews to count as a pattern.

Then copy `templates/patterns-analysis.md` to `01-discovery/synthesis/patterns-analysis.md` and run the five whys on your strongest pattern. Commit both files.

Optional, if stuck: `resources/affinity-mapping.md` and `resources/five-whys-root-cause.md`.

## 1:15 to 1:40. Checkpoint 3: verdict and script v2

Open `templates/prediction-trial.md` as a worksheet (do not commit it). Count how many logs support the "we are wrong if" line from your problem hypothesis, and how many raised your predicted problem without being asked. Pick one verdict: confirmed, weakened, or killed. Write it as a new line at the bottom of `DECISIONS.md`, in this exact shape:

```
Wk3 · Prediction "<your we-are-wrong-if line>" verdict: killed · evidence: 01-discovery/interview-logs/01-host-oni.md, 03-host-oni.md · owner: ZA
```

Also write the same verdict, one line, at the bottom of `01-discovery/problem-hypothesis.md`.

Then copy `templates/interview-script-v2.md` to `01-discovery/interview-script-v2.md`. Every surprise that appears in 2 or more logs earns a new question. Every question that only produced compliments is cut. Commit.

Optional, if stuck: `resources/testing-your-prediction.md` and `resources/evolving-the-script.md`.

## 1:40 to 1:50. Studio

Book interviews. Milestone 1 needs 6 real logs by the Week 4 lab; the full rubric is in `MILESTONE-1-RUBRIC.md` at the repo root. Read it today, not next week.
