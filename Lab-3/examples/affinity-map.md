## Pattern 1

**Pattern:** Hosts fear the double-book more than the commission.

**Interviews behind it:** 01, 03, 05, 07

**Quotes:**
- "In August I don't sleep. I check the phone at 3 in the morning because I'm afraid I sold the same room twice." (03)
- "Last August I think I sold room two twice in the same week. I only found out when the second family called from the road." (01)
- "When I'm full I call Manana next door before I answer anyone, so I don't promise what I don't have." (05)
- "The notebook is by the phone. I check it twice a day. Still, twice this season." (03)
- "We gave the money back to one family. That is the worst thing, giving money back." (07)

## Pattern 2

**Pattern:** English replies arrive too late for foreign guests.

**Interviews behind it:** 02, 06

**Quotes:**
- "My son writes the English answers. When he is in Tbilisi, nobody answers." (02)
- "A Polish family wrote on Monday. I understood on Wednesday. They had already booked somewhere else." (06)
- "I can read a little. Writing back, no. I wait for him." (02)

## Pattern 3

**Pattern:** Repeat guests get lost between apps.

**Interviews behind it:** 04, 08

**Quotes:**
- "At checkout she said, we were here three years ago, you made us khachapuri. I did not remember. I felt terrible." (04)
- "Some write on Booking, some on WhatsApp, some on Instagram. I don't know who is who." (08)
- "If I could see, this one stayed before, I would give the good room." (04)

## Not yet a pattern

- "A discount for people who come back, that would be nice." (02). One mention, from a prompted question. Watching.
- "My cousin in Shovi sends me his overflow, I send him mine." (05). Could become an informal-network pattern if it recurs.
