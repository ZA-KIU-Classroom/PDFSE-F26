# Worked Example: the Sakhli Founder's Pool

Three statements the Sakhli founder pooled in her Lab 1, plus one that failed. Yours must come from your own eyes; copying these fails the desk test by definition.

```
AUTHOR: Keti
WHO I watched: my aunt, who runs a 6-room guesthouse in Oni
STRUGGLE I saw: double-booked twice in one August; a family from
Warsaw stood in the yard with suitcases while she phoned neighbors
COST I can name: two lost bookings, a week of apologizing, and she
now refuses walk-ins out of fear
```

```
AUTHOR: Keti
WHO I watched: the same aunt, at 23:00 on a Tuesday
STRUGGLE I saw: typing replies to an English inquiry word by word
through a translation app, while two more inquiries sat unanswered
COST I can name: guests book elsewhere when answers take a day; she
answered 3 of 12 foreign inquiries within 24 hours that week
```

```
AUTHOR: Keti
WHO I watched: her neighbor Dato, who also hosts
STRUGGLE I saw: keeps repeat guests' names and phone numbers in a
paper notebook; last winter he could not find the family who
promised to return "next summer"
COST I can name: zero repeat bookings last season from guests who
said they would come back
```

**Why these pass.** Each WHO survives the room test (a specific aunt, a specific neighbor). Each STRUGGLE survives the camera test (you could film the yard scene, the midnight typing, the notebook). Each COST is a number or a consequence you can point at. And notice: no apps, no solutions, three different problems. She did not pick one until Lab 2.

## The one that failed

```
AUTHOR: Keti
WHO I watched: tourists in Georgia
STRUGGLE I saw: it is hard to find authentic guesthouses online
COST I can name: bad experiences
```

Torn down: "tourists in Georgia" fails the room test. "Hard to find" was never observed, it was assumed, so it fails the camera test. "Bad experiences" is not a cost anyone can point at. This is a desk statement wearing hiking boots.
