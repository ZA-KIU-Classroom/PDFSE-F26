# Lab 1 · Team Foundation
**Week 2 · Live online · 2 hours**

This page is the whole lab. If something is not on it, it is not required.

## What you leave with
A team of 2 to 4, a signed contract, a pooled set of problems, and a live repo. This is the foundation layer of Milestone 1 (10 pts, Week 4).

## Bring
Your 3 problem statements from Week 1 (WHO / STRUGGLE / COST). Write them before the lab, not during it. No statements means nothing to pool, and your team pools today.

## The rhythm
| Clock | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Recap and Q&A. Two random screen-shares: show your 3 problem statements. |
| 0:15 | Live demo: I tear down a bad interview script, line by line. Watch what dies and why. |
| 0:35 | Guided build. Three checkpoints below. |
| 1:20 | Studio: outreach lists. I take the queue. |
| 1:50 | Homework, on screen. |

## Checkpoint 1 (by 0:50): Team formed, contract drafted
2 to 4 people. No solo teams. Copy `templates/team-contract.md` and fill it together, with real names, real times, and real channels. A contract with blanks is not a contract.

One clause is non-negotiable and pre-written in the template:

> No member may answer a question about the product with "that is not my role."

Accountabilities say who makes sure something happens. They never say who is allowed to care. Any member can be asked about any part of the product, in labs, at milestones, and by the Demo Day judges.

## Checkpoint 2 (by 1:20): Problem pool committed. Zero solutions.
Every member's 3 statements go into one file: `00-foundation/problem-pool.md`, in the shape shown in `templates/problem-statement.md`. A team of 3 pools 9 problems. Read each other's out loud. Ask one question per statement: did you see it, or did you imagine it?

Do not pick a problem today. Last year, teams that married their first idea in Week 1 spent discovery collecting compliments for it. Lab 2 picks, with the Four Filters, on evidence. Teams that converge today will be asked to reopen.

## Checkpoint 3 (by 1:45): Repo live
Accept the GitHub Classroom link (pinned in Teams). Commit the contract and the pool to `00-foundation/`. House rule for the semester: if it is not committed, it did not happen.

## Studio: start the outreach
Lab 2 launches your interview sprint, and interviews take longer to schedule than you think. Copy `templates/outreach-tracker.md`, list 5 or more people connected to your pooled problems, and send the first 2 messages before the lab ends. There is a message you can adapt inside the template.

## Files in this folder
`templates/` : the contract, the problem statement shape, the outreach tracker. `examples/sakhli-problem-pool.md` : a real pool, annotated, including one statement that fails. `optional-deep-dive/` : stretch material, never required, never graded.
