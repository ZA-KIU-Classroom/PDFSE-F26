# Lab 1 Homework

**Deliverable:** Signed team contract and pooled problem statements committed to `00-foundation/`. Each member: 3 problem statements from personal observation.
**Deadline:** Before your Week 3 lab.
**Submission:** Commit to your team repo (GitHub Classroom link in Teams).
**Points:** Feeds Milestone 1 (10 pts, Week 4). Watch-check for the Week 3 recording is due before your lab.

Do not pick your team's problem yet. That happens in Lab 2 with the Four Filters. Teams that converge this week will be asked to reopen.

That is the entire assignment. The team contract template is `templates/team-contract.md`. The problem statement shape is `templates/problem-statement.md`. Your Week 1 statements count toward your 3; sharpen them against the quality bar in the template before you commit them.
