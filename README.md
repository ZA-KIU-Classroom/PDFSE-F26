# Product Development for Software Engineers
**Kutaisi International University · Fall 2026 · Zeshan Ahmad**

You no longer play a role. You run a loop: notice a real problem, hack a test together, put it in front of real people, read the evidence honestly, then iterate or pivot. This course is fifteen weeks of running that loop for real: a team of 2 to 4, a problem you personally observed, a product deployed to the public internet before the exam break, measured while you study, and pitched to invited industry judges at a hybrid Demo Day in Week 15.

One sentence is banned all semester, and it goes in your team contract in writing: "that is not my role."

## How a week works
Weeks 1 and 2 are in person. From Week 3:

| Step | What | When |
|---|---|---|
| Recording | Chaptered lecture with pause-and-do prompts | At least 48 hours before your lab |
| Watch-check | 3 quick judgment questions per recording | Before your lab (best 8 of 10 count) |
| Live lab | 2 hours online: recap, live demo, guided build with checkpoints, studio, homework on screen | Weekly |

Every lab includes two random 2-minute screen-share demos of your team's current product state. Anyone can be called. Stay demo-ready.

## The semester
| Weeks | Phase | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 4 | Discover | Interviews, synthesis, evidence-based problem. Milestone 1 (Wk 4). |
| 5 to 7 | Ship | North star metric, MVP boundary, deployed thin slice with live analytics. Hard gate at Wk 7. |
| 8 to 9 | Exam period | Midterm on campus. Your product stays live and collects real signal. |
| 10 to 13 | Learn and sharpen | Read the data, run experiments, AI evals, unit economics, strategy. Milestone 2 (Wk 11). |
| 14 to 15 | Integrate and pitch | Consolidation week, then Demo Day. Milestones 3 and 4. |

## How this repo works
- `Week-1/` is your entry ticket. Start there.
- `Lab-N/` folders appear before each lab. The README in each folder IS the lab: if something is not on that page, it is not required.
- Every lab folder has a `HOMEWORK.md` with the deliverable, deadline, submission link, and points in the first ten lines. The same text is posted in Teams. No decoding, no surprises.
- `templates/` are meant to be copied into your team repo and filled with your real names, numbers, and evidence. `examples/` show the Sakhli worked example; copying its answers fails by design. `optional-deep-dive/` is stretch material, never required.
- Your team's work lives in your own team repo (GitHub Classroom link in Teams), not here.

## House rules
1. **If it is not committed, it did not happen.**
2. **The Evidence Rule (from Week 3):** every product decision ships with the evidence behind it, one line in your `DECISIONS.md`.
3. **No problem-picking before Lab 2.** Teams that converge early will be asked to reopen.
4. **AI tools are welcome and logged** in `docs/ai-usage-log.md`. Fabricated interviews, quotes, or data end badly.
